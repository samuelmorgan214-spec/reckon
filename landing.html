How it works

A prediction market is a running tally of what informed people are willing to back their judgement on. It moves before the news does.

Markets, never polls

Every number comes from a real traded market. Where nothing is priced, we say so plainly rather than inventing a figure.

Sourced and timestamped

Each price carries where it came from and when it last moved. Every snapshot is kept, so the record can be checked later.

Information only

Reckon takes no bets and links to no operators. It is a media product about what markets imply, nothing more.
